# THE DEATH OF ERNIE MIRANDA

THESE DAYS, A "MIRANDA CARD" IS STANDARD EQUIPMENT FOR AMERICAN POLICE --A SMALL PIECE OF PAPER THAT'S AVAILABLE FOR QUICK REFERENCE WHEN AN ARREST IS MADE. MOST ARRESTING OFFICERS READ DIRECTLY FROM IT: "YOU HAVE A RIGHT TO REMAIN SILENT; ANYTHING YOU SAY MAY BE USED AS EVIDENCE AGAINST YOU IN COURT; YOU HAVE A RIGHT TO HAVE AN ATTORNEY REPRESENT YOU DURING QUESTIONING AND ALL STAGES OF THE COURT PROCEEDING; IF YOU CAN'T AFFORD A LAWYER, THE COURT WILL APPOINT ONE FOR YOU AT COURT EXPENSE,"

ERNIE MIRANDA'S CONVICTION WAS OVERTURNED BY THE SUPREME COURT, BUT HE WAS EVENTUALLY RETRIED ON THE SAME CHARGES AND CONVICTED, HE GOT OUT OF PRISON IN 1972, AND THIS WEEKEND HE WAS STABBED TO DEATH IN A BARROOM FIGHT OVER A CARD GAME,

THE MAN WHO IS CHARGED WITH KILLING ERNIE MIRANDA WAS ARRESTED JUST A FEW MINUTES LATER, THE FIRST THING THE POLICE DID WAS TO ADVISE HIM OF HIS RIGHTS. GH/PBM
